# LLM Obsidian v2.6.5

Version 2.6.5 makes the unattended lifecycle exact-HEAD and event-driven,
bounds finalization to five immutable cycles, and activates the previously
preview-only Split contract after a separately proven Stability Gate. It does
not add a scheduler, broaden provider or permission policy, increase model
budgets, or make merge/release an automatic Split effect.

## Exact-HEAD lifecycle

One `ReviewAttempt` now owns one exact product HEAD and ends terminally at
approval, rejection, changes requested, attention, failure or cancellation.
The active path cannot rearm that provider session on a different HEAD.
Historical cross-HEAD records remain inspectable and cleanable but return the
typed `legacy-cross-head-resume-disabled` disposition before provider input.

Join also adds immutable `VerificationAttempt` identity:
`parent + verification profile + exact HEAD + attempt index`. A typed
coordinator mechanism-flake decision may create attempt 1 on the same HEAD,
without an empty commit or model/provider call. Attempt 0 remains durable; a
second retry or any decision/identity drift stops at attention. A changed HEAD
continues through the distinct fix-and-resubmit path.

## Typed provider events and bounded finalization

Provider observation is normalized into a closed `ProviderEvent` vocabulary
with exact provider, operation, run, generation and cursor identity. Delivery
receipts are irreversible. Time, terminal text, screen changes and physical
resource disappearance cannot assert business completion. Durable close events
repair the stale `exiting` case without adding another writable authority.

Claude print and Codex exec ephemeral adapters share one conformance contract:
fixed argument compilation, subscription preflight, schema validation,
capability checks and durable receipts. An adapter failure cannot silently
switch providers, paid access or hidden interactive mode.

Finalization reserves one of five immutable cycles atomically. The assigned
provider is primary; a separately typed, freshness-bounded availability record
may allow the independent route. Unknown or stale availability stops rather
than probing a provider. The optional policy is additive to PipelineSpec and
ordinary pipelines retain their previous semantics.

## Governed Split activation

`$split` still produces a canonical zero-effect manifest preview. Only the
explicit `$split --dispatch` branch may activate an approved plan. Each child
is bound to disjoint owned files and evidence, dependencies, an existing
workspace dispatch request, a registered pipeline/route and frozen time/token
ceilings.

`split-runner.py` validates with zero effects, then calls the existing dispatch
adapter for at most one dependency-ready bounded wave. Immutable launch
receipts make restart idempotent. Later waves require exact approved
resource-free terminal receipts. Attention, failure, cancellation, conflict,
budget drift, stale HEAD or open resources stops the graph before another
child/provider effect.

Join is deterministic and resource-free: original launch receipts, terminal
receipts and current child HEADs yield one manifest-order integration list. It
does not merge, push, tag, publish or release.

Recovery remains effect-exact. Dispatch log context links bind the exact
context-path stem to their display title. The strict repair planner may accept
one typed exact binding, but rejects path, target, source or hash drift. A
durable reap completion receipt can settle only its matching pending
`reap-finalize` effect; replay, reuse and cross-task identity fail closed.

The four component heads were adopted in A-B-C-D order without squashing or
rewriting their review evidence. A and D had durable product evidence but ended
in contained lifecycle attention; their providers were not replayed. Stability
Gate S passed at exact clean pre-activation HEAD
`023f69320b0833f8aac33a69f03d387ea2e485c1` before Split was enabled.
