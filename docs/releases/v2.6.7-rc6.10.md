# LLM Obsidian v2.6.7 RC6.10

RC6.10 adds one durable, display-only timing boundary for identity-bound
reviewer sessions. Active reviewers keep live elapsed time; terminal reviewers
freeze their own accepted callback-bound duration. Harness lifecycle authority
and the existing dashboard presentation remain unchanged.

## Reviewer interval

- After the exact callback envelope validates, the review transport publishes
  one owner-only record under the existing `review-data` boundary.
- The record binds schema, owner, root, parent reviewer operation, review-round
  operation, run, axis, exact review-input start, callback-observed completion,
  and accepted callback digest.
- Publication is immutable. An identical replay accepts the existing canonical
  bytes; malformed, symlinked, partial, or conflicting evidence is preserved as
  unavailable and never changes callback acceptance or lifecycle processing.

## Dashboard projection

- A nonterminal reviewer continues to use its exact liveness interval and
  renders `elapsed`.
- A terminal reviewer renders `duration` only when one complete review-round
  child, its OperationStore callback identity, and every timing-record identity
  agree. The aggregate review phase is the maximum of those same validated
  child intervals.
- Legacy absence, malformed JSON, partial records, symlinked paths, identity or
  digest drift, non-finite or negative timestamps, future timestamps, reversed
  intervals, and nonterminal callback evidence never become terminal timing.
- Palette, layout, row ordering, expansion policy, root scope, finding counts,
  and unrelated timer output remain byte-stable.
