# LLM Obsidian v2.6.7 RC3

RC3 is the final stabilization candidate for the bounded
`engineering/change` corridor introduced in RC1 and made observable per root
in RC2. It adds no new pipeline or dashboard feature. It packages the narrow
repairs discovered by live acceptance and proves the candidate sequentially
before exercising two concurrent opposite-runtime roots.

## Lifecycle stabilization

- Executor and reviewer startup separate durable process ownership from
  semantic input acknowledgement. Prompt delivery and one bounded recovery are
  identity- and digest-bound and never replay completed provider work.
- Changed product HEADs require a fresh exact-HEAD verification receipt before
  another review can start.
- Review-drive recovery recognizes live bound rounds across their nonterminal
  states and scopes failure receipts to the exact cycle.
- Malformed review-resolution output receives at most two corrections from the
  same live executor. Exhaustion remains typed attention rather than an
  unbounded loop.
- Archive and reap rendering preserve valid vault syntax even when reviewer
  prose resembles a wikilink or a bounded log excerpt reaches its size cap.
- Terminal success authority accepts the real request-exit cleanup effect and
  proves empty provider, process, supervisor, surface, and review resources.

## Release boundary

RC3 remains a bounded stabilization claim, not a claim that every legacy or
custom Harness pipeline is generally reliable. Unknown or ambiguous states
still fail closed with typed attention. The root-scoped terminal observer is
read-only, external to lifecycle authority, and closed by the user.

This release is published as the prerelease tag `v2.6.7-rc3`.

The final independent Fable High implementation review approved exact
candidate `9b12e3453a6cd81da7361e32b7cc60aa3c3187d0` with three minor findings and
no material finding. The repair-chain auditability finding was closed by the

neither carries unexplained release authority.
