# LLM Obsidian v2.6.6 RC1

This release candidate removes the compatibility lifecycle that remained after
2.6.5 and narrows review authority to exact attempts, typed provider events and
exact Split ancestry. It is intentionally deletion-first: the branch removes
20,895 lines overall while adding bounded regressions and immutable evidence.

## Lifecycle authority

- A terminal review attempt is authoritative only for its exact product HEAD.
  Changed HEADs reserve a fresh bounded attempt; historical attempts are
  inspect/archive/cleanup-only.
- Human incident UUIDs, local paths, digests and decision prose no longer
  compile into executable recovery authority. Cross-HEAD rearm, drift,
  mechanism-recovery and post-fresh compatibility modules are removed.
- Reviewer liveness is observational. Time, stable screen state,
  `turn-stopped`, and dead/unknown process status cannot send input, submit a
  callback or restart a provider. Until a supported runtime exposes an
  authenticated turn-complete event, callback recovery is attention-only.
- Bounded review diff packets truncate UTF-8 safely at byte boundaries.

## Split identity

Dispatch resolves an immutable base commit before creating a child worktree.
Every normalized child dispatch request must carry that same sealed SHA before
pipeline compilation or any launch effect. The SHA is persisted through task
metadata, Split policy, manifest, launch and terminal receipts, evidence
projection and deterministic Join. Moving the diagnostic branch now fails
before worktree/provider effects instead of silently changing ownership;
unrelated history is rejected by ancestry proof.

Replay keeps the same authority without requiring optional raw fields. Before
sealing recovered launch evidence, Split reads the actual durable child task
contract and proves its request, worktree, branch, vault, policy and base. A
missing or conflicting contract stops before a write. Public `start` output now
includes `base_sha` and round-trips directly into Join even after the mutable
source ref is removed. A mismatched receipt still fails against the manifest,
and the accepted child is never launched a second time.

## Evidence and candidate ceiling

Slices A-G retain an exact-commit retrospective RED/GREEN/focused ledger; it is
not represented as independently generated execution proof. A typed accepted-
deviations artifact records that boundary and assigns immutable per-slice
receipts to RC2 instead of manufacturing historical proof. Integration binds named
exact-attempt, provider-event and Split-base traces, the audit manifest,
coverage floors and a real RC1 review dogfood trace. That trace preserved two
accepted callbacks and reached a terminal resource-free inventory; the
original envelopes, receipts, gate and operation snapshots are archived under
a digest-bound manifest. Its
automatic-ingestion failure is explicitly deferred to RC2. The first release
candidate found two unobserved zero-caller adapters, and the first deep review
then found stale terminal approval, synthetic Stop authorization, and stale
evidence bindings. Those RC1 blockers are fixed with focused regressions.

The authoritative broad gate, complete candidate ledger and final bounded
review are emitted only after the last product commit as owner-controlled
exact-HEAD sidecars. The review input binds the final receipt path/digest and
accepted-deviations artifact. This avoids
the previous pattern where committing evidence advanced the very HEAD that the
evidence claimed to cover.
