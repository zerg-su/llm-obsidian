# LLM Obsidian v2.6.6 RC4-fix2

RC4-fix2 is a bounded dashboard and lifecycle-evidence patch over
`v2.6.6-rc4-fix1`. It packages an external read-only Harness dashboard and the
repairs found while dogfooding that view. It does not change the pipeline DSL,
review topology, provider routing, permission model, or durable lifecycle
authority.

## External Harness dashboard

- The companion terminal view projects the real compiled pipeline, frozen
  executor and reviewer routes, step visits, bounded loops, parallel lanes,
  recent terminal history, and recent typed issues.
- It remains outside Harness ownership: it cannot resume, cancel, reconcile,
  signal, or mutate lifecycle state.
- The newest genuinely running pipeline stays visible inside a continuously
  redrawn terminal viewport. Older attention-only programs use compact
  summaries and every omitted row is represented by a truthful hidden count.
- A restrained semantic palette distinguishes complete, running,
  waiting/review, retry, attention, and reviewer/model tokens. `--no-color`,
  non-TTY, JSON, and `--once` output preserve their plain compatibility
  contract.

## Exact evidence and recovery

- Missing exact-HEAD verification is visible attention unless a durable child
  identity proves that the current attempt is still running; stale children
  cannot suppress the issue.
- Persisted dashboard markers that alias the caller surface are retryable and
  never reused or closed.
- Exact tracked-tree gate evidence fails closed when the candidate changes.
- Dashboard policy retains a leaf dependency boundary instead of importing the
  provider/supervisor runtime.
- The declared code-quality and scope gates are green without a new accepted
  deviation.

## Verification boundary

The final candidate passed the focused dashboard, lifecycle, documentation,
scope, quality, and exact-HEAD gates. One independent Opus high review was the
final review boundary; it found one important dependency-boundary issue and
three minor test/documentation issues;
all material findings were resolved before final evidence was rebound at HEAD
`db2c6da57eeba7ebd3c4e204c744ae8d6711efb7`.
