# LLM Obsidian v2.7.8

Version 2.7.8 is a bounded stability candidate for two defects exposed by the
2.7.7 release runs. It does not introduce a scheduler, lifecycle or store
owner, provider fallback, retry budget, public schema, dependency, or replay
path.

## One-shot protected-research cleanup

- Research prompts delivered in the provider argv retain their exact prompt
  pointer as digest authority. The runtime publishes input acceptance through
  the existing generation-bound provider-event stream without sending the
  prompt again.
- After the callback broker accepts the exact result, the runtime publishes
  the accepted payload digest as `result-published`. The ordinary process and
  resource observations then complete the existing sequence:
  `provider-started`, `input-accepted`, `result-published`, `process-exited`,
  `resource-closed`.
- The research fetch can therefore close and create its synthesis child
  without another prompt, provider send, or network fetch. Missing or
  mismatched generation, prompt, result, callback, or resource evidence stays
  fail-closed.

## Proven-stale custom escalation suppression

- A delayed executor `mechanism-failure` raise is classified as
  `suppressed-stale-post-self-heal` only from the frozen custom definition,
  exact task/store/controller identity, contiguous accepted receipt chain, and
  canonical next request.
- The predecessor child must be complete, resource-free, effect-free, and
  bound to the accepted callback identity and payload digest. Its callback
  outbox must be absent, while the exact successor child must already exist in
  `awaiting-callback` with no resources, effects, or accepted callback.
- The proven case has zero escalation, attention-pointer, notification, or
  surface-send effects. Every absent, malformed, stale, foreign, non-custom,
  non-mechanism, or otherwise ambiguous case retains ordinary escalation.
