## Governance

The ledger distinguishes terminal `fixed-rc2`, `deleted-rc2`, and
`implemented-rc2` outcomes from `defer-post-rc2` and `not-in-scope`. The TDD
finding is explicitly deferred evidence-only because its current semantic
contract remains green; no unneeded skill change was made. Updates to completed
or deferred plans in the user-owned Swarm vault remain coordinator-owned and
occur only after the accepted product handoff.

The full verification profile is the release gate. Review is a confirmatory
judgment over the same exact HEAD, not a substitute for the gate. Any material
post-review product change creates at most one replacement candidate and must
repeat both exact bindings.

The first exact candidate `cc904862369e14ccf7aaec19ecf01fe7b229f1e9`
correctly published no evidence after the coverage gate found a trace-owner gap
caused by the classic test deletion and two uncovered public Store rejection
branches. `RC2.COVERAGE_REHOME_AFTER_DELETION` records the narrow test-only
repair. The later scoped candidate `348c50ab640e97e419d1effce84acc1d2b582c19`
exposed four bounded correctness gaps during exact Codex review and one stale
documentation snapshot during Fable review; the defect ledger binds their
dispositions. These intermediate descendants are not release candidates. The
next clean HEAD that passes the full immutable profile is the single permitted
replacement candidate.
