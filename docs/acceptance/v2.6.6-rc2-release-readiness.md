## Governance

The ledger distinguishes terminal `fixed-rc2`, `deleted-rc2`, and
`implemented-rc2` outcomes from `accepted-deviation-rc2`, `defer-post-rc2`,
and `not-in-scope`. The TDD
finding is explicitly deferred evidence-only because its current semantic
contract remains green; no unneeded skill change was made. Updates to completed
or deferred plans in the user-owned Swarm vault remain coordinator-owned and
occur only after the accepted product handoff. The two bounded deviations are
recorded in `v2.6.6-rc2-accepted-deviations.json`; neither is presented as
product evidence.

The full verification profile is the release gate. Review is a confirmatory
judgment over the same exact HEAD, not a substitute for the gate. The plan did
not define whether failed unpublished gate executions consume its one
replacement-candidate budget. RC2 records that ambiguity rather than rewriting
history, permits one final post-review repair candidate, and forbids another
hidden fix loop. Future plans count full-profile executions mechanically.
