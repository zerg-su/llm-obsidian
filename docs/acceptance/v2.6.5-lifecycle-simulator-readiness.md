## Boundary

The simulator proves deterministic logical composition and crash-prefix
recovery against fake external adapters. It does not replace live provider,
cmux, process-signal, filesystem/power-loss, or network dogfood. Push, tag,
publish, deploy, and release remain separately authorized actions and are not
performed here.
