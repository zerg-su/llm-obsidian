  Contract and design.
- Planning stays read-only; architecture ambiguity returns to design.
- Every slice retains exact ownership, consumes/produces, RED, minimal GREEN,
  refactor seam, focused verification, and Outcome evidence mapping.
- Approval and complete evidence coverage remain completion conditions; a
  written plan or green local check cannot establish the Outcome.

  bindings remain exact and fail closed.

  background-shell fallback is permitted.

  provider/review/reap processes.

  remain protected.
