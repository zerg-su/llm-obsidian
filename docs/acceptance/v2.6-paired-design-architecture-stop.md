# LLM Obsidian 2.6 paired design architecture stop

## Decision

The third frozen post-change design forward test is **not achieved**. Its
automatic simple review found two important findings at task HEAD
`19ee018f8420c7bca9b86d9ac47dfac9c56cce90`:

- `holistic-executor-ownership-gap`: the proposed executor was
  `RuntimeSessionManager`, while `scripts/harness/runtime_worker.py` owns the
  live provider handle and performs the current nudge/restart effects;
- `holistic-prototype-evidence-missing`: the committed result reported stdout
  without preserving independently runnable prototype source.

The task was stopped without a fourth local repair, resolution round, merge, or
reap. Its document-only commit remains isolated on
`task/llm-obsidian-2-6-paired-design-post-third` as negative evidence.

## Architecture review

The failure is not evidence that the 2.6 Outcome Contract or the general
Workstream A design rules are wrong. The useful general rules are still:

- begin with owned boundaries and test seams;
- preserve the incoming Outcome Contract without semantic drift;
- name interfaces and their owners;
- prefer vertical slices and trace acceptance to evidence IDs;
- stay read-only until implementation or another effect is approved.

The failed sequence added increasingly fixture-specific prescriptions for
reservation order, durable budgets, participating records, mutable effect
inputs, and recovered reservations. Those prescriptions were derived from one
callback-recovery fixture rather than from the frozen `A-DES-01` finding. They
made the skill longer while still allowing the model to assign effect
execution to a component that does not own the live handle. They therefore do
not meet the plan's behavioural-acceptance rule and are removed from 2.6.

This is an evidence-driven rollback to the general Workstream A boundary, not
a claim that effect idempotency is unimportant. Concrete recovery designs must
derive ownership from inspected code and may record domain-specific invariants
in their ADR or plan; they must not turn one fixture's architecture into global
skill policy.

## Validation boundary

One fresh paired-design run may be launched only after this architecture
decision and only from the cleaned release candidate. It is a new validation
cycle for the rollback, not another fix iteration in the stopped task. It must
reuse the frozen contract, fixture, model alias, verification profile, and
review budget. Passing requires all four evidence IDs to be independently
established and no regression versus baseline. Failure stops the design skill
change from release acceptance; it does not authorize another automatic skill
patch.
