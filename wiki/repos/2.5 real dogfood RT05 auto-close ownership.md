# 2.5 real dogfood RT05 auto-close ownership

## Outcome

RT05 reproduced the observed `auto_close_expected=1,left_open=1` record and proved it was a pre-v2.3 telemetry false positive, not a current cmux leak or duplicate RT02 cleanup exposure. A resumable provider return was labeled from static unattended policy; the same task later completed and closed its exact surface. Current v3 dispatch keeps surface ownership on the parent operation while typed child phases own no surface.

## Scoped changes

- `a3d55a8` adds a deterministic ownership regression: an `awaiting-callback` parent rejects terminal cleanup, retains its exact surface, and closes no surface.
- `3ca6aac` adds the durable bounded diagnosis, RT01/RT02 boundary, no-close decision, and exact replacement recommendation in `docs/acceptance/v2.5-real-rt05-auto-close-ownership.md`.
- No runtime close path, public reporting contract, schema, event log, vault page, or unrelated surface was changed.
