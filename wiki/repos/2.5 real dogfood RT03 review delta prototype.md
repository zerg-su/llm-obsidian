# 2.5 real dogfood RT03 review delta prototype

## Outcome

Tested whether same-session review verification can replace its rebuilt packet with a machine-built delta packet. The bounded prototype proved structural completeness and deterministic identity, but not live-model review-quality equivalence. Production review behavior therefore remains unchanged.

## Evidence

- Added `prototypes/rt03-review-delta-packet.py` and ADR `docs/decisions/rt03-same-session-review-delta-packet.md` in commit `99d56159ab66ed8b07e141ac8b42e26e0a8f7270`.
- All six structural checks passed: deterministic packet construction, original ContextPacket binding, exact reviewed/resolved HEAD binding, multi-commit resolution coverage, detection of the HEAD-only evidence gap, and a bounded oracle verdict matching the exact resolved tree.
- The fixture's HEAD-only change set exposed one file; the reviewed-to-resolved range exposed both changed files. The generated packet was 2,941 bytes.
- Coordinator host-side `make test-harness` passed at the exact committed HEAD and was accepted as the required scoped verification evidence.

## Decision

Keep the current same-session gate and exact product-HEAD inspection. If a later live paired experiment shows no material-finding loss, prefer an anchored delta supplement containing the original manifest digest, reviewed and resolved HEADs, exact findings, the complete resolution-range diff, and verification evidence. Do not promote the prototype without that live quality evidence.
